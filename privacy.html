Privacy Policy

Effective October 7, 2026

The short version. We collect what the game needs: your first name, the character you pick, your sign-in, the games you play and the proofs you take. Your photos and videos are seen only by the players of the game you sent them to. We do not sell your data, we show no ads and we do not track you across other apps. You can delete your account from the app at any time.

Who we are

Dibs is published by Mathieu Askamp, France ("we", "us"). We are the controller of your personal data. For any question or request about your data, write to us through the contact form.

Dibs asks for your camera, microphone and photo library only when you take or pick a proof. We do not use advertising identifiers or ad networks, and we never track you across other apps or websites.

Who sees what

  • Players of your games see your first name, your character, the cards you play on them, your proofs in that game, your reactions and your points. Proofs are never public and never shown outside the game they belong to.
  • Our service providers process data only to run Dibs, under contract: Supabase (database, file storage, sign-in, servers, hosted in the European Union, Paris region), Apple (Sign in with Apple, push notifications, payments), Google (Sign in with Google), Amplitude (usage analytics, United States) and Vercel (this website).
  • Authorities, only when the law requires it.

We do not sell or share your personal data for advertising, as defined by the California Consumer Privacy Act or any other law.

Legal bases (EU and UK)

  • Contract: to provide the game you sign up for (account, profile, games, proofs, Dibs+).
  • Legitimate interest: security, abuse prevention, fixing bugs and usage analytics to improve the game.
  • Consent: push notifications and device permissions, which you can withdraw in your device settings.
  • Legal obligation: keeping purchase records when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

  • Your account, profile and proofs: until you delete your account. You can find past games and their proofs in the app while your account exists.
  • When you delete your account, we delete your sign-in, profile, push tokens and the photos and videos you posted. In games you played with others, your name is replaced with "Deleted player" so their scores still add up.
  • Reports: up to 12 months after they are closed.
  • Technical logs: kept by our hosting provider on its own short retention cycle.
  • Usage analytics: kept while we use them to improve the game. Ask us through the contact form and we delete yours.

Your rights

You can access, correct, export or delete your data, object to or restrict its use, and withdraw consent. Most of it you can do in the app (Profile). For anything else, use the contact form; we answer within 30 days. If you are in the EU, you can also complain to your data protection authority (in France, the CNIL, cnil.fr). California residents have the right to know, delete and correct their data, and we do not discriminate against anyone who uses these rights.

Delete your account

In the app: Profile, then Delete account. Full steps on the delete your account page. Deleting your account does not cancel a Dibs+ subscription billed by Apple: cancel it in your iPhone Settings, then Apple ID, then Subscriptions.

Security

Data travels encrypted (HTTPS). Proofs are stored in private storage and are only served to the players of their game. Access to the database is restricted to what each player is allowed to see.

Adults only

Dibs is not for anyone under 18. If you believe a minor uses Dibs, tell us through the contact form and we will delete the account.

International transfers

Our database and files are hosted in the European Union. Apple, Google and Vercel may process some data in the United States under the safeguards provided by law, such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

Changes

If we change this policy, we update the date above and tell you in the app when the change matters.
