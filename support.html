Support

Answers to common questions, and a way to reach us.

How do I play?

Create a game, share the code with your friends or your partner, and play a card on someone. They have until the timer ends to prove it with a photo or a video. The faster they do it, the more points. In the app, tap Rules for the full list.

How do I join a game?

On Play, tap I have a code and type the six-digit code, or open the invite link your friend sent you.

What are jokers?

You get two in each game. NO U sends the card back to whoever played it. NOPE cancels it, and nobody scores.

What does Dibs+ include?

Every deck, including Spicy 18+, Date night, Long distance, Roommates and Party, 30-day games, up to 12 players and several games at once. 3 days free, then $4.99 per week or $29.99 per year. Only the host needs Dibs+: everyone in their game plays with it for free.

How do I cancel Dibs+?

On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Dibs. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date. For a refund, go to reportaproblem.apple.com.

I bought Dibs+ but it does not show.

Open the Dibs+ screen and tap Restore at the bottom, or open Profile and tap Restore purchases. Use the same Apple ID you bought it with.

Someone posted something they should not have.

Open the proof and tap Report or Block. To report or block a player, open Scores and tap the player. We review every report. If someone is in danger, contact local emergency services first.

Who can see my photos and videos?

Only the players of the game you posted them in. Read the Privacy Policy.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Profile, then Delete account. See delete your account.
